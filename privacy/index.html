Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Calendar and Tasks application ("the App") operates. The App is a third-party client designed to help you manage your Google Calendar and Google Tasks. Your privacy is our highest priority.

Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

The Mobile App

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data, events, or tasks to the developer or any third parties. No analytics, tracking tools or advertising SDKs are integrated into the App.

The Website

Our official landing page (hosted on GitHub Pages) uses Google Analytics solely for domain verification purposes and basic website traffic monitoring. Google Analytics may process non-personally identifiable technical information (such as cookies, browser type, and masked IP addresses). This data is handled by Google and is entirely separate from the mobile App's operation.

Permissions

To function correctly, the App requires you to sign in with your Google account. You must grant permission to access your basic profile, email address, Google Calendar API, and Google Tasks API. Without these permissions, the App cannot bypass the authorization screen and will not function, as it relies entirely on these services to operate.

These permissions are used solely to allow you to view, create, and edit your events and tasks within the App's interface.

Children's Privacy

Because the App does not collect, store, or process any user data on external servers, we do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

Security

All communication between the App and Google services is encrypted using secure protocols (HTTPS). The App never sees or stores your Google password, as authentication is handled entirely through Google's secure OAuth 2.0 standard. Your data remains protected by Google's infrastructure.
